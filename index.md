Agentcraft is where the thinking happens: rough ideas, proposals, experiments, failures, and reflections.

The Field Guide is the shelf of durable tools: selected material that is useful enough to revisit, reuse, or share.

```text
Agentcraft = where thinking happens
Field Guide = where durable lessons are surfaced
```

## Operating promise

- Markdown is the source of truth.
- Rough notebook entries do not need to be site-ready.
- Published pages should be explicit about uncertainty and maturity.
- The goal is leverage and better judgment, not automation theater.
