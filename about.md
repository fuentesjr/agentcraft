Agentcraft is a public lab notebook for practical AI work.

The Field Guide is the curated layer: cheatsheets, playbooks, guides, patterns, and templates that are useful enough to revisit, reuse, or share.

The boundary matters:

```text
Lab notebook = messy, exploratory, honest
Field Guide  = curated, navigable, durable
```

Rough notes do not need to be site-ready. Publishing is an explicit promotion step.
